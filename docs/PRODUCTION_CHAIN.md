# Production chain

`model proposal → creator films → footage approval → Chatterbox narration → audio listening/approval → video assembly → final review/export approval → optional separate publishing`

The footage and narration approvals are independent gates. Every approval applies to the exact file hash reviewed. Editing approved footage or regenerating audio creates a new candidate that needs approval. Final export approval is also tied to the exact assembled file. The app does not publish automatically.

Trend observations are optional prompt context. No web-content acquisition or article sourcing is part of this production chain.
